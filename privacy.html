1. Overview

NEXORA ("we", "us", or "our") provides an intelligent email monitoring, classification, task management, and WhatsApp alert notification hub. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you connect your Google Account and utilize NEXORA.

2. Information We Collect

When you use NEXORA and link your Google Account via Google OAuth, we access and process:

  • Google Account Identity: Your email address, name, and unique Google ID for authentication.
  • Gmail Message Data: Message headers (sender, recipient, subject, date), message bodies (plain text / HTML), and message labels (Inbox, Unread, Spam, Trash) required for mailbox synchronization.
  • Email Attachments: File metadata and text content from supported documents (e.g. PDF, text) solely to detect deadlines and action items.
  • Phone Number: Your configured WhatsApp destination number used solely for dispatching urgent email notifications and task reminders.

3. How We Use Your Information

The data accessed through Google APIs is used strictly to provide the core services of NEXORA:

  • Synchronizing your unread and incoming emails onto your secure NEXORA dashboard.
  • Classifying emails by category (Work, Finance, General) and priority (Urgent, Important, Normal).
  • Generating concise summaries and detecting deadlines or action items.
  • Sending automated WhatsApp alerts for incoming messages and due task reminders.
  • Drafting and dispatching user-approved or automated contextual email replies.

4. Google API User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use)

NEXORA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not sell your Google user data to any third parties.
  • We do not use or transfer your Google data for serving advertisements, including personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not use your email data to train generalized AI models. AI inferences are performed strictly in the context of providing summaries and replies for your own workspace.
  • Human access to your email data is strictly prohibited unless you provide explicit consent for debugging or if required for security purposes by law.

5. Data Storage and Security

All OAuth tokens are encrypted using AES-GCM encryption before database persistence. All communications between NEXORA, Google APIs, and Twilio WhatsApp gateways are encrypted in transit via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

6. Revocation and Deletion of Data

You can revoke NEXORA's access to your Google Account at any time by clicking Disconnect Google in your NEXORA Settings or directly through your Google Account Permissions. Upon disconnection, stored OAuth credentials are wiped, and automatic mailbox monitoring is terminated.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy requests regarding your data, please contact the administrator via your NEXORA workspace settings.
